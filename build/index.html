    07 / Questions

    A few things worth asking.

    Still curious? Say hello at hello@frst.example — a real person reads it.

    Is frst another todo list?
    Not really. A todo list tells you what's possible; frst tells you what fits. Everything you add gets a place on the clock, and anything that doesn't fit is deliberately pushed to later instead of quietly haunting you.
    What if my morning blows up?
    Then the day blew up — that happens. There are no broken streaks, no red badges and no "you're falling behind" emails. Hit replan after lunch and start from wherever you actually are.
    Which calendars does it work with?
    Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar are supported today. frst only reads free/busy times and never moves your meetings without asking.
    Where does my data go?
    Your tasks stay in your browser in this demo. In the full app they're encrypted in transit and at rest, never sold, and exportable as plain text whenever you want to leave.
    Can I cancel whenever?
    Yes. One click, no retention flow, no "are you sure" maze. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata for unused months.
